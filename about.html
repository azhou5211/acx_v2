Who We Are

Deep experience. Engineered for what's next.

ACX Industries is a U.S.-based advanced materials and hot-zone solutions company serving high-temperature manufacturing industries.

Built on decades of experience in crystal growth, hot-zone engineering and advanced carbon materials, we combine materials expertise, component engineering and process understanding to create solutions that improve performance, reliability and operating value.

We look beyond the individual part to understand how materials, geometry and process conditions interact within the complete hot zone — from thermal conductivity and expansion at the material level, through component geometry and heat-flow paths, to the temperature distribution and process conditions the hot zone experiences in service.

Engineering the Hot Zone as a System

From material properties to process performance.

Hot-zone performance is determined by the interaction of materials, geometry, thermal behavior and process conditions. ACX combines practical crystal-growth experience with thermal modeling and material engineering to evaluate the complete system — not just individual components.
