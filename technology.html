Patented Graphite Heater Technology

Eliminating a common heater failure point.

The connection between heater elements is typically secured with multiple screws. A complete heater can contain up to 24 screw holes.

~92% of premature heater retirements are associated with cracking at screw-hole connection points

Field observation under customer operating conditions.

Why the connection fails

01

Screw Holes

Up to 24 openings create local stress concentration.

02

Thermal Cycling

Room temperature to ~1650°C, repeatedly.

03

Expansion Mismatch

More than 2× difference between heater and screw materials.

04

Surface Contact

Microscopic unevenness produces localized loading.

From conventional connection to ACX screwless design.

Conventional Design

Stress concentrates around the screw-hole connections during repeated thermal cycling.

ACX Patented Screwless Design

A bonded connection removes the drilled holes and eliminates the conventional screw-induced stress mechanism.

ACX Solution Patented screwless heater construction + in-house high-temperature graphite bonding technology

Don't reinforce the failure point. Eliminate it.
